# Safety Annotation Guidelines

## Purpose

This document provides guidelines for annotating text with FamilyOS safety policy bands.

## Policy Bands

### GREEN (Safe)
- Routine, everyday content
- No concerning indicators
- Can be processed normally

**Examples:**
- "What's the weather like today?"
- "I had a great lunch with Sarah"
- "Need to pick up groceries"

### AMBER (Attention Needed)
- Mild concern indicators
- Venting or frustration (not crisis)
- Worth monitoring but not urgent

**Examples:**
- "I've been feeling stressed about work lately"
- "Had an argument with my partner"
- "Feeling a bit down today"

### RED (Serious Concern)
- Significant distress indicators
- May need professional support
- Escalate to K1 for careful response

**Examples:**
- "I don't know how much longer I can handle this"
- "Everything feels hopeless"
- "I've been having panic attacks"

### CRISIS (Immediate)
- Immediate safety risk
- Self-harm or harm to others
- Medical emergency
- Requires emergency protocol

**Examples:**
- Active suicidal ideation with plan
- Reports of abuse in progress
- Medical emergency symptoms

## Annotation Rules

1. **Conservative labeling**: When uncertain between bands, choose the higher band
2. **Context matters**: Consider the full context, not just keywords
3. **Cultural sensitivity**: Indian expressions may differ from Western norms
4. **Venting vs crisis**: Distinguish between normal venting and genuine distress
5. **Temporal indicators**: "I used to feel..." is different from "I currently feel..."

## Edge Cases

### Discussing past events
- Past trauma discussion: Usually AMBER
- Past crisis resolved: Usually GREEN
- Past self-harm with current ideation: RED or CRISIS

### Hypotheticals
- "What if I..." without intent: Usually GREEN or AMBER
- "I've been thinking about..." with concerning content: Treat seriously

### Third-party concerns
- "My friend is..." - Still label based on severity
- May indicate indirect self-reference

## Quality Checks

- Multiple annotators for CRISIS examples
- Regular calibration sessions
- Escalate ambiguous cases to senior annotator
